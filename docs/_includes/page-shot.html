{%- comment -%} One of the two photographs that stand in the prose rather than in a slideshow: the side panel under "Choosing what to translate", and the Options page under the glossary section. Both exist once per language, because both are the extension's own UI — see the note in `tests/guide-shots.mjs`. The picture is picked by the page's own `lang`, so the ten pages carry the same line and differ only in `alt`, which is prose and belongs to the translation. Named rather than pathed: a page that spells its own filename is a page that can point at a picture in the wrong language, which is the thing this include exists to make impossible. 1280×800 is what `FRAME` in `tests/framed-shot.mjs` takes and what `tests/assets.test.js` holds these files to. The attributes are here so the column reserves the room before the picture arrives; `height: auto` because the guide's column is narrower than the picture on every screen it is read on, and the attributes alone would squash it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign src = "/assets/" | append: include.name | append: "-" | append: page.lang | append: ".png" | relative_url -%}
