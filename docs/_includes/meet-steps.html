{%- comment -%} The Google Meet slideshow, under "Using with Google Meet". Same shape as `install-steps.html` and the same CSS — one step showing at a time, a picture and a marker on the one thing the step asks for — with its own words in `_data/meet.yml` and its own radio group, so a reader can be on step three of the install and step six of this one at the same time. The tab strip is in two rows, one per phase: everything before the call, then everything with a meeting already on screen. The heading that breaks the row is the `phase` written on the step it starts at, and the tabs and panes are picked out by index classes rather than by `nth-child` — a heading in the middle of the strip makes the nth tab and the nth child two different things. `figures` is positional, as it is in the install slideshow: the nth step gets the nth picture. Three are photographs of the side panel, taken per language by `tests/guide-shots.mjs` and measured into `_data/shots.yml`, because the panel is the extension's own UI and a reader in Korean is looking at a Korean panel. The other four are drawings, and the only words in any of them are `meet.google.com` and `Interpretab (translated)` — neither of which is translated anywhere. Meet's own labels are deliberately not drawn: they would have to be sourced per language the way `tools/aistudio-strings.mjs` sources AI Studio's, and a mistranslated button in a picture sends a reader hunting for something that is not there. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign copy = site.data.meet[page.lang] | default: site.data.meet.en -%} {%- assign steps = copy.steps -%} {%- assign figures = "phones,meettab,meetmic,join,meetstart,device,talk" | split: "," -%}
