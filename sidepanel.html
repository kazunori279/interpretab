The translation comes out of your speakers. Getting it into a Meet or Zoom microphone needs a virtual audio device — an extension cannot do it. With both directions on, wear headphones: speakers feed the translation straight back into the mic.

For two people sharing this microphone: whichever of the two languages it hears, it speaks the other. Out loud, through your speakers, which is the point — but it means the interpreter can hear itself, so keep the microphone away from them. This mode waits for each turn to finish, so it is a little slower than Simultaneous, and it is the only one the glossary applies to.

Both directions run two concurrent Live sessions, so the API cost is roughly double.
